# Semester 1
In Semester 1, the focus was on learning the fundamentals of IT. 
We worked with basic networking, storage solutions and virtualization to gain an understanding of how a common home environment operates.

### This is what we needed to have to pass:
- Create a guide for a customer on the most suitable internet subscription.
- Show a user how to connect to a virtual machine with TrueNAS.
- Show someone outside of your network he can access a Synology NAS or a TrueNAS

Looking back, these tasks seem relatively simple. 
However, at the time they introduced many new concepts and challenged us to understand networking, remote access and storage solutions for the first time.

Besides the technical aspects, an important part of Semester 1 was getting to know my classmates, making new friends and becoming familiar with the teachers.
